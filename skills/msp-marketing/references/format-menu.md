# Format Menu: The Output Flavors

Every piece of content is one of these formats. Lengths are shipped example defaults (see
SKILL.md Setup Decisions) drawn from web-writing norms; tune them once real performance data
exists. All formats follow msp-brand voice and the SKILL.md style guardrails.

The social channel list, its phases, and the posting mechanics live in msp-social. This file
owns what each post looks like. If you apply the owner-visibility rule in msp-social, no format
names the owner as the owner.

The platform limits in formats 8 to 13 (and the limits added to formats 3, 4, and 6) are limits
checked October 2026 against the leading platform in this category; check your platform's
current limits before you rely on them. Each one names its source tier: official (the
platform's own docs), scheduling tool docs (the leading social scheduling tool's docs), or
third-party. The recommended lengths are shipped example defaults, like the rest of this menu.

---

## 1. Full Resources Article (the anchor format)

**For:** the resources/blog section of {{DOMAIN}}. The canonical home of every topic.
**Length:** 600-900 words.
**Structure:**

- **Title:** benefit- or question-led, under 60 characters, no clickbait. Good: "How to Spot a
  Phishing Email Before You Click." Bad: "You Won't Believe What Hackers Are Doing Now."
- **Opening (2-3 sentences):** the reader's situation or problem, in their words. No throat-
  clearing, no "in today's world."
- **Body:** 3-5 sections with plain-language subheadings. Short paragraphs (2-4 sentences).
  Use a numbered list when giving steps, prose otherwise.
- **"What to do about it" section:** every article ends with concrete action the reader can take
  this week, even without hiring anyone.
- **CTA:** one, from the CTA library, as a natural closing paragraph.

**SEO checklist (required for this format only):**

1. One target phrase a business owner would actually type ("how to spot phishing email," not
   "email threat vectors"). Use it in the title, one subheading, and the first 100 words. Never
   stuff it.
2. Meta description: 140-155 characters, plain English, includes the phrase, promises the payoff.
3. 1-2 internal links to relevant pages on {{DOMAIN}} (services page, standing-offer page, or a
   prior resource) with natural anchor text.
4. Title options: deliver 2-3 title candidates with the draft.

## 2. Short Resource / Quick Tip

**For:** lighter website posts, professional-network articles, or a fast-turnaround piece.
**Length:** 150-250 words.
**Structure:** one problem, one fix, one CTA. A single tip done well, not a compressed article.

## 3. Professional Network Post

**Status:** available by default. If you apply the owner-visibility rule in msp-social, hold
professional network posts and do not name the owner as the owner.

**For:** the company page and the owner's personal profile on your professional social network
(personal profiles typically outperform company pages; post to both, personal first).
**Limit:** 3,000 characters for members and pages. A URL counts as 24 (scheduling tool docs).
About 210 characters show before "see more" on desktop and about 140 on mobile (third-party).
**Length:** 100-200 words, first line under 80 characters (it's the hook before the fold).
**Structure:** hook line → the insight or micro-story in 2-4 short paragraphs → one question or
soft CTA. No hashtag walls; 0-3 relevant hashtags. No links in the body when avoidable (comment
or reshare with the link); if a link is needed, one only.
**Voice note:** slightly more personal than website copy. First person is welcome ("A client
asked me this week...", details anonymized per msp-sales case-study rules).

## 4. Local Business Listing Post

**For:** the company's local business listing profile, which feeds local search.
**Length:** 80-120 words visible; front-load the point in the first sentence.
**Structure:** local angle or timely hook → one useful point → CTA button text ("Learn more" /
"Call now") pointing at {{DOMAIN}}. Always attach an image (brand-styled per msp-brand; a brand
icon tile works when nothing better exists).
**Media:** images or video. The listing platform's post editor accepts both (checked October
2026). Whether your social scheduling tool can send video to the listing is not confirmed. Use
one image until you test a video post through the scheduling tool.
**Limit:** 1,500 characters (the listing platform's post editor, checked October 2026). The
80-120 word target sits well under it.
**Platform notes:** the listing platform archives posts older than 6 months (official). It can
reject a post that contains an unverified phone number, so use the msp-brand phone number
({{PHONE}}) only.

## 5. Newsletter Blurb

**For:** the monthly client/prospect newsletter (example default; see Setup Decisions).
**Length:** 50-100 words per item.
**Structure:** tease the article's payoff, link to the full resource. Written fresh, not the
article's first paragraph.

## 6. General Social Post

**For:** the company's general social network page (an example phase 1 channel in msp-social).
**Length:** 40-120 words. Warmer and more casual than the professional network, same guardrails.
**Limit:** no practical limit. The leading scheduling tool accepts up to 5,000 characters
(scheduling tool docs).
**Media:** the pack image or video when one fits.

## 7. Security Advisory (handoff format)

When the topic is an active threat and the audience is **existing clients**, this is not
marketing: load msp-client-comms and use its security advisory template. This skill may then
adapt the same topic into a public resources article (formats 1-4) with the client-specific
details removed. Public version educates; it never reveals anything about clients'
environments.

## 8. Microblog Post

**For:** the company's microblog platform account (an example phase 1 channel).
**Limit:** 280 characters for a standard account. Emoji count as 2. Every URL counts as 23,
whatever its real length (official). Bio: 160 characters (official).
**Length:** one or two sentences, under 240 characters with the link.
**Structure:** the single most useful point, stated plainly → link to the article. 0-2
hashtags. Attach the pack image when one fits.

## 9. Photo-First Social Post (example phase 2)

**For:** the company's photo-first social platform account, once your social scheduling tool
plan covers it.
**Limit:** 2,200 characters, 30 hashtags, 20 @ tags (official). The leading scheduling tool
counts 2,196 and counts each line break as 2 (scheduling tool docs). About 125 characters show
before "more" (third-party).
**Media:** required. Every post needs an image or a video (official).
**Length:** 60-150 words.
**Structure:** the hook inside the first 125 characters → the point in 2-3 short paragraphs →
one CTA → 3-5 relevant hashtags at the end.

## 10. Short Video on the Video-Sharing Platform (example phase 2)

**For:** the company's channel on the video-sharing platform (short-form video), once your
social scheduling tool plan covers it. Uses the licensed marketing pack videos.
**Limit:** video up to 3 minutes. Title 100 characters. Description 5,000 bytes, not characters
(official).
**Length:** title under 60 characters. Description of 1-3 sentences plus the article link.
**Structure:** plain-language title that names the problem → description that says what the
viewer learns → link to the full resource.

## 11. Short-Form Video Platform Post (example phase 2)

**For:** the company's short-form video platform account, once your social scheduling tool plan
covers it. Uses the licensed marketing pack videos.
**Limit:** caption 2,200 characters on a video post (official). The leading scheduling tool
allows 4,000 on photo or text posts (scheduling tool docs). The platform sets the video length
cap per account (official API). Bio: 80 characters (third-party).
**Media:** required. Every post needs media.
**Length:** one or two sentences.
**Structure:** a plain hook that matches the video → 2-4 relevant hashtags.

## 12. Conversation Platform Post (example phase 2)

**For:** the company's conversation platform profile, once your social scheduling tool plan
covers it.
**Limit:** 500 characters and a maximum of 5 links. In a text-only post the first link becomes
the preview card. Media is optional (official).
**Length:** 1-3 short sentences, under 400 characters.
**Structure:** a conversational point → one link to the article. No hashtag lists.

## 13. Neighborhood Platform Post (manual)

**For:** the company's neighborhood platform business page, if the owner starts one. The owner
posts each approved item by hand on its date.
**Limit:** no business post limit found. The developer docs list a body limit of 8,192 and 10
attachments for their content APIs (official, partial).
**Length:** 60-150 words.
**Structure:** a local, useful item (a scam warning in {{SERVICE_AREA}}, a power-outage tip, a
free workshop) → what a neighbor can do about it → a soft CTA. Helpful neighbor, not an ad.

## Repurposing Chain Reference

Default chain per SKILL.md: Full article → one post per active social channel (example phase
1: local listing, general social, microblog) → newsletter blurb once it launches. The active
channel list lives in msp-social. Write each piece fresh for its channel. Label each piece and
separate with `---` when delivering.

## Sources for platform limits

Confirm each limit in the platform's own help and developer docs before you rely on it.
